What n8n Automation does

The purpose of the application, in plain terms.

Most business work involves the same small actions over and over: reading an enquiry, copying it into a spreadsheet, sending a confirmation, creating a calendar event, chasing a payment. The n8n Automation application lets a user describe that sequence once, as a workflow, and then run it automatically whenever it is needed.

A workflow is a chain of steps. Each step reads from a service, transforms the data, or writes to another service. n8n Automation executes the chain, records what happened, and reports any failure. Users build their own workflows in the dashboard at n8n.abokor.shop; the application itself is the engine that runs them.

The application is used by Abokor Tech and by clients and teams who have been given access to a workspace, or who connect their own accounts. It is a business tool, not a social network, and it does not display advertising.

What you can automate

Typical building blocks available inside a workflow.

Multi-step workflows

Chain several services together, branch on conditions, loop over records and retry failed steps.

Google Workspace

Read and send Gmail, append rows to Google Sheets, create Calendar events and work with Drive files.

AI steps

Send text to an AI model as one step of a workflow — for summarising, classifying or drafting a reply.

Schedules and triggers

Run every morning, every hour, when a webhook is called, or when another system sends an event.

Notifications

Send results to WhatsApp, Telegram, email or an internal endpoint so people hear about it immediately.

Execution history

Every run is recorded with its inputs, outputs and errors, so a failure can be found and fixed quickly.

How it works

From sign-in to a running automation in four steps.

STEP 01

Sign in

Open the dashboard and sign in to your workspace with your email address and password.

STEP 02

Connect accounts

Authorise the services a workflow needs. For Google, you approve the exact scopes on Google's own consent screen.

STEP 03

Build the workflow

Add the steps you need, map the fields between them, and test the flow with sample data.

STEP 04

Activate and monitor

Turn the workflow on. It runs on schedule or on trigger, and every execution appears in the history.

Google account access and permissions

What n8n Automation asks for, why it needs it, and what it does with it.

Google access is granted through Google's OAuth 2.0 flow. Nothing is read or written until you approve the consent screen, and the application requests only the scopes needed for the specific steps a workflow uses. If you decline, the workflow simply cannot use that service.

Google integrations and why access is needed
Google serviceWhy the access is needed
Account and profileIdentify which Google account is connected and show it in the dashboard.
GmailRead a message a workflow processes, or send a reply the workflow prepares.
Google SheetsRead, append or update rows as part of a business process.
Google DriveList, read, upload or create the files a workflow handles.
Google CalendarCreate or update events and read availability when scheduling.
Google DocsRead or generate documents as a workflow output.
What we never do: we do not sell Google user data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train our own models. Access is limited to the purposes you approved, and you can revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The full detail is in our Privacy Policy, including the Google API Services User Data Policy and Limited Use requirements.

Common questions

Do I need an account to read this page?

No. This page, the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service are public. An account is only needed to use the dashboard and run workflows.

Why does n8n Automation ask for Google permissions?

Because a workflow can only read an email, write a row to a sheet or create a calendar event if you have authorised that access. The request is shown by Google before you approve it, and only the scopes a workflow needs are requested.

Can I revoke access later?

Yes. Remove the connection in your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or delete the credential inside the dashboard. Workflows that depend on it will stop working.

Where is the application hosted?

The dashboard runs at n8n.abokor.shop on a private server operated by Abokor Tech, published through Cloudflare over HTTPS.
