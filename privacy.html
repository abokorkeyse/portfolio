02Purpose of the n8n Automation application

n8n Automation is a self-hosted workflow automation platform, available at n8n.abokor.shop. It lets a user connect services together and define automated workflows ("flows") that move data between them — for example: reading an incoming email, appending a row to a spreadsheet, creating a calendar event, generating a document, sending a notification, or calling an AI model.

A workflow only does what its author configures it to do. Google user data is requested and used for one reason: to perform the action the user has explicitly configured in a workflow — for example, to read a message the workflow processes, or to write a record to a sheet the user chose.

The application is used by Abokor Tech itself and by clients, partners and authorised users who are given access to a workspace or who connect their own accounts.

03Information we collect

We collect only the categories of information described below.

3.1 Google account information when you authorise access

When you connect a Google account, Google displays an OAuth 2.0 consent screen. If you approve it, Google returns to the application: your Google account identifier, your email address, your display name, your profile picture and locale (where the granted scopes include profile information), and an OAuth access token together with a refresh token. The tokens are stored so the connection keeps working without asking you to sign in again.

3.2 Email addresses and profile information

Your email address and profile details are used to identify which account is connected, to display that account in the dashboard, to notify you about workflow failures or service changes, and to reply when you contact support. We do not use them for advertising.

3.3 Data processed through connected Google APIs

When a workflow executes, it may read or write data covered by the scopes you granted — for example Gmail messages and labels, Google Sheets rows, Google Drive files, Google Calendar events, or Google Docs content. That data is handled by the workflow and is typically passed to the destination the workflow author configured (another sheet, a database, an email, an AI service, and so on). We do not routinely read, review or mine this content; it is processed to run the workflow you asked for.

3.4 Workflow execution data and logs

The platform records technical information about how workflows run: workflow identifiers and names, start and end times, execution status, node inputs and outputs (which may contain fragments of the data being processed), error messages and stack traces, and dashboard access events such as timestamps, IP address and browser user agent.

05How Google user data is accessed and used

Google user data is accessed only after you authorise it, and only for the purposes described in this policy and shown to you on the consent screen:

  • To run the workflows you configure — reading and writing the specific records a flow needs to complete its task.
  • To maintain your connection — using refresh tokens to obtain new access tokens so a flow keeps working without repeated sign-in.
  • To show you status — displaying which account is connected, whether a credential is still valid, and whether an execution succeeded.
  • To troubleshoot — investigating a failure you report, using the execution logs of the affected workflow.
  • To keep the service secure — detecting abuse, unauthorised access or attempts to compromise an account.
  • To comply with the law — where we are legally required to retain or disclose information.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, for building advertising profiles, for selling or renting it, or for training our own machine-learning models. We do not allow humans to read your Google data except where you have asked us to help with a specific issue, where it is necessary for security or abuse investigations, or where the law requires it.

06How information is stored and protected

The application runs on a private virtual private server provided by a third-party hosting provider, currently located in the United States, and is published through Cloudflare. Workflow definitions, credentials and execution records are stored in the platform's database on that server. OAuth client credentials and tokens are stored in the platform's encrypted credential store.

Technical and organisational measures we apply include:

  • HTTPS/TLS for all traffic between your browser, Cloudflare and the server.
  • Encrypted storage of credentials and tokens using the platform's credential encryption key.
  • A host firewall that exposes only the ports required for the service; the database is not publicly reachable.
  • Administrative access limited to authorised administrators, using key-based SSH authentication.
  • Regular updates of the operating system and the application, and periodic backups of configuration and data.
  • Access to execution data restricted to the users of the relevant workspace and administrators.
Honest limitation

No service can guarantee absolute security, and we do not claim any security certification or formal audit. If we become aware of a breach that affects your data, we will notify affected users without undue delay and take steps to contain it.

07Data sharing and third-party services

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information is shared only in the following situations, and only to the extent needed:

  • Google APIs. Requests are sent to Google to perform the actions you authorised — for example reading a message or writing to a sheet.
  • Services you connect inside a workflow. A flow may send data to services that you (or the workspace owner) configured, such as OpenAI or another AI provider, an SMTP or email provider, WhatsApp or Telegram messaging APIs, a database, or a webhook endpoint. Those transfers are defined by the workflow itself; you control which destinations are used.
  • Infrastructure providers. Our hosting provider stores the server and its backups; Cloudflare provides DNS, CDN, TLS and tunnel connectivity and processes request metadata such as IP address and user agent.
  • Contact form delivery. Messages sent through the contact form on abokor.shop are delivered by FormSubmit.co to our email inbox.
  • Website analytics. abokor.shop uses Google Analytics to collect aggregate usage statistics (see section 11).
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by applicable law, a valid legal request, or to protect the rights, safety and security of users, the public or Abokor Tech.
  • Business transfer. If the service is transferred to another operator, data may be transferred as part of that operation, subject to this policy.

Third-party services process data under their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing the policy of any service you connect to a workflow.

08Data retention and deletion

We keep information only as long as it is needed for the purposes described above:

  • OAuth tokens and credentials — retained while the connection is active, until you disconnect the account, delete the credential, or ask us to delete it.
  • Workflow definitions — retained until you delete them.
  • Execution records and node data — retained according to the instance's execution-data settings, which can be configured to prune older executions and can be deleted manually at any time.
  • Server and access logs — retained for a limited period for security and troubleshooting, then rotated or deleted.
  • Contact messages — retained for as long as needed to answer your enquiry and keep a record of the conversation.
  • Backups — retained on a rolling basis and overwritten as new backups are created.

How to delete your data

  1. Revoke the application's access in your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. This immediately invalidates the tokens Google issued to us.
  2. Delete the corresponding credential and workflow in the n8n Automation dashboard.
  3. Or email abokor445@hotmail.com with the Google account address concerned and ask us to delete the data. We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days, and we will confirm when it is done.

Some records may be retained longer where we are legally required to keep them, or where they are needed to resolve a dispute or prevent abuse. In that case we keep only what is necessary and restrict its use to that purpose.

12Your rights and consent withdrawal
