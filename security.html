1. Overview & Commitment

At Abokor Tech, security, user privacy, and data protection are fundamental priorities. We welcome feedback and reports from security researchers, ethical hackers, and users to help identify and remediate potential security vulnerabilities in our websites and services.

2. How to Report a Vulnerability

If you believe you have discovered a vulnerability in abokor.shop or related infrastructure, please notify us immediately via email:

Primary Security Contact:

abokor445@hotmail.com

Preferred Languages: Somali (so), English (en)

Standard File: https://abokor.shop/.well-known/security.txt

Please include the following details in your report:

  • URL, component, or parameter affected.
  • Type of vulnerability (e.g. XSS, authentication bypass, header misconfiguration).
  • Step-by-step reproduction steps or proof-of-concept (PoC).
  • Potential impact and remediation suggestions.

3. Scope

In Scope:

  • https://abokor.shop and primary static endpoints.
  • Application routing and API endpoints under abokor.shop.

Out of Scope:

  • Denial of Service (DoS / DDoS) attacks or network-flooding attempts.
  • Social engineering, phishing, or physical attacks against employees or infrastructure.
  • Third-party integrations outside our control (e.g. external hosting providers, CDN core edge).

4. Responsible Disclosure & Safe Harbor

If you act in good faith and adhere to the following principles during your research:

  • Avoid accessing, modifying, or destroying user data.
  • Do not disrupt our production systems or availability.
  • Give us reasonable time to investigate and remediate the issue before publicly disclosing any details.

We commit to:

  • Acknowledging receipt of your report promptly (typically within 48 hours).
  • Investigating and verifying the vulnerability in a timely manner.
  • Not initiating legal action against researchers who comply with this policy in good faith.
